Exact rules and what is approximated

Let u = shoulder angle / 95°, v = elbow angle / 150°, and h = lift. The state is (u,v,h), with −1 ≤ u,v ≤ 1 and 0 ≤ h ≤ 1. Arm lengths are 1 and 0.8 workbench units.

E = (cos 95°u, sin 95°u)
P = E + 0.8(cos(95°u+150°v), sin(95°u+150°v))

The probe P must be 0.20 to 0.65 units from target center (1.30, 0.40). The low post is a vertical radius-0.17 shaft up to lift 0.55, with a rounded cap ending at 0.72. For each finite arm link, let d be its exact shortest overhead distance to (0.65, 0.50). Clearance requires d² + max(0,h−0.55)² ≥ 0.17². A link touching the boundary is allowed. Only the probe is forbidden from the gray center; it is a target rule, not a physical obstacle to the links.

These inequalities define every valid point, including between mesh triangles. The colored 3D wall is sampled from them. Its genus and smooth appearance are numerical mesh observations; the exact rule boundary has some corners where conditions meet.

The magenta route is three straight segments in control space: lift from 0.35 to 0.80, change both angles, and lower to 0.35. A conservative bound on how fast each rule score can change between sampled route points certifies that every point of these continuous segments is valid.
