What was fitted and checked

All 1,334 images are verified against the Python renderer. Center (14,14) and height 19.75 px stay fixed. Every layout retains the width/lean mesh connections and source order.

The global view fits all original image-to-image Euclidean distances. The two path-aware views additionally fit short path distances, the cosine of the angle between the two local control steps, and triangle areas measured from the original vectors. Those are explicit geometric measurements, not pixel colors or PCA axes.

The analytic fitting gradient is checked against finite differences. Optimization has a finite iteration limit and does not certify the best possible drawing. The area penalty discourages collapse and local inversion; remaining reversals are counted rather than hidden. A reversal count cannot detect all possible global overlap.

Two vectors can always be drawn with their exact lengths and mutual angle in 2D. Showing that pair exactly does not establish that the entire surrounding sheet fits into one plane without distortion. This is a finite sampled drawing, with no exact global flattening or continuous-decoder claim.
