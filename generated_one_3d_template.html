What is preserved, and what is not?

Inputs: a Sobol sample across all five allowed generated controls, five reference paths, and the 32 corner states. The algorithms receive only the resulting 784-pixel images. This is the controlled renderer, not the real MNIST dataset.

Neighbor overlap is the fraction of each point’s 15 nearest images in 784-pixel distance that remain in its 15 nearest displayed points. Global order is Spearman correlation for sampled image pairs. Distance error is sqrt(sum((pixel distance − a × 3D distance)²) / sum(pixel distance²)), using the best single scale a.

No method shown is a continuous injective representation of the full five-dimensional family in three Euclidean coordinates. The images and local scores are retained so apparent contacts and separation in 3D can be checked against the original pixels. The cloud is a finite sample, not a reconstructed 3D boundary surface. Thumbnail pixels are rounded to 8-bit for display only; the embeddings and measurements used the renderer's full pixel values.
