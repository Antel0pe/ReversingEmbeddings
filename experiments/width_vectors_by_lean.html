Read the difference carefully

The width vector says where widening adds ink. The change in that vector compares two widening responses: red means the response is larger here than at the reference lean; blue means it is smaller. Blue in that comparison does not mean widening removed ink.

The whole min-to-max vector is the sum of the small width-step vectors. Those steps can turn along the sweep, so one endpoint vector does not describe every intermediate image.

Scope and resolution

This page explores the allowed width and lean ranges at one fixed center and height. It is a sampled two-knob slice of the five-knob family. Vectors are finite image differences, not analytic derivatives. Rendering uses 16 subrows and float32 storage; displayed measurements subtract and accumulate with higher precision.
