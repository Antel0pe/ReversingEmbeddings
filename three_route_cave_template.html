Why this one is exact 3D

The state is already the point (x,y,z) in ordinary space. There is no dimensional reduction: Euclidean distances and angles between states are exactly their 3D distances and angles. The equation below defines membership continuously. The triangles only approximate the visible wall to draw it.

Mathematical definition and mesh accuracy

M = { (x,y,z) ∈ ℝ³ : S(x,y,z) ≥ 0 }, where S = 0.015 log[∑j=15 exp(sj/0.015)].

Let q = π(x+2.05)/4.1. Three scores describe winding tubes: sj = rj(x)² − [y−yj(x)]² − [z−zj(x)]² − 0.12(x/2.2)10.

(y₁,z₁) = (1.30 sin q + 0.12 sin 3q, 0.35 sin 2q + 0.12 sin 4q)
(y₂,z₂) = (−1.25 sin q + 0.10 sin 2q, −0.35 sin 2q + 0.11 sin 3q)
(y₃,z₃) = (0.25 sin 2q + 0.10 sin 4q, 1.42 sin q − 0.13 sin 3q).

r₁ = 0.36 + 0.04 sin²(4q) + 0.11 exp[−((x+0.55)/0.45)²]
r₂ = 0.36 + 0.06 sin²(3q+0.4) + 0.09 exp[−((x−0.35)/0.4)²]
r₃ = 0.35 + 0.04 cos²(4q) + 0.10 exp[−((x+0.15)/0.4)²].

The chamber scores are s₄ = 0.66² − (x+2.05)² − y² − z² and s₅ = 0.70² − (x−2.05)² − y² − z². The smooth maximum S joins these five regions.
